# Buyer hesitation notes template

Use this lightweight worksheet when reviewing why a shopper might hesitate on a product page. [Open AngleCue](https://anglecue.app/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=public-assets) for the live buyer-question workflow.

![Buyer hesitation and product insight illustration](../assets/anglecue-insights.png)

## Review one product

```text
Product/page: ______________________________________________

What is the buyer trying to decide?
____________________________________________________________

What could make them pause?
[ ] unclear benefit       [ ] missing proof
[ ] confusing offer       [ ] trust or delivery concern
[ ] comparison question   [ ] other: ________________________

Evidence on the page:
____________________________________________________________

Smallest useful improvement:
____________________________________________________________

How will we know it helped?
____________________________________________________________
```

Keep observations tied to visible page evidence and real buyer questions. Do not invent customer feedback or treat a hypothesis as a measured result.
